## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are technical recruiters, hiring managers, and founders reviewing portfolios quickly. They need to understand in 30–60 seconds that Atharv Mittal is a software engineer who can move between security rigor and product execution as a single skill. Secondary audience: peers, clients, and collaborators.

### Brand Personality
- **Cinematic gallery** — each project is presented like an exhibit in a dark, intentional space.
- **Security × product** — the core story is that technical depth and product instinct are not separate; they reinforce each other.
- **Confident, precise, slightly dangerous** — like a well-lit museum of things that could break things.

Three-word personality: *precise, cinematic, dual-wielded*.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark-first, near-black surface with a single warm accent (electric orange) and one cool secondary (cyan) used sparingly.
- Massive typography as the interface; small mono labels as metadata.
- Generous negative space; restrained color; one strong visual moment per section.
- Motion serves narrative: reveals, transitions, and scroll-linked pacing — never random blur or zoom.

### Design Principles
1. **Story first, effect second.** Every animation must advance the narrative or reveal information.
2. **One belief.** The page should leave the reader believing security and product are one skill here.
3. **Exhibit, don't decorate.** Projects are presented as case studies with problem → solution → impact.
4. **Restraint is the flex.** Use one accent color, one type pairing, one signature interaction.
5. **No AI slop.** No gradient text, no glass cards, no side-stripe borders, no generic icons above headings.
